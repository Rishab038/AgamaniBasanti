# Agamani Basanti — Owner's Guide

Everything you do day-to-day, in one page. Keep this somewhere easy to find.

**Your dashboard:** https://admin.agamanibasantifashion.com
Log in with your email and password. It works on your phone's browser too.

---

## Every day (2 minutes)

Open the dashboard. The **Today** page shows everyone at a glance:

- **Checked in today** — who has come to work.
- **Need your decision** — days where something looks off (came very late,
  left early, only one of app/fingerprint worked). Tap the Attendance page,
  open that person's day, and pick what it should count as: Present, Half
  day, Overtime, or No pay. **Your choice is what decides their salary.**
- **Advance requests** appear at the bottom — Approve or Not now, one tap.
  The worker's app tells them your answer instantly.

That's it for a normal day.

## Every month (salary day)

1. Open **Salary**. Pick the month.
2. If a yellow warning shows at the top, deal with it first — it means
   some days still need your decision, or some staff have no salary set.
3. Press **Generate draft**. Check the list — every name, every amount.
   You can fix attendance and press it again as many times as you like.
4. When the numbers look right, press **Confirm & freeze**. Advances are
   cut automatically. Each worker sees their own attendance in their app.
5. **Export CSV** gives you the sheet for your accountant.

## When a new person joins

1. Tell them to install the app and tap **"New staff? Join"**.
2. Give them the **shop code** (Settings page → Joining codes — each shop
   has its own code; give the code of the shop they work at).
3. They fill their name, phone and a 6-digit PIN of their choosing.
4. They appear on your **Staff** page — press **Approve**.
5. On the same page, set their **salary** (and for PF staff, the salary
   break-up: Basic, HRA, Conveyance, Washing) and their **shift timings**
   if you want late-coming tracked for them.

**Adding someone yourself instead:** Staff page → **Add worker**. Fill in
their name, mobile number and a 6-digit PIN, then pick the **staff type**:

- **Normal** — you enter one monthly salary figure.
- **PF** — you enter the break-up (Basic, HRA, Conveyance, Washing); the
  page adds them up and shows the total. PF, ESI and professional tax are
  worked out from these at salary time.
- **Contract** — like Normal.
- **No pay no work** — you enter a daily wage.

Shift timings are optional here too. Give them the mobile number and PIN
you chose, and they can log in straight away.

## When someone leaves

Staff page → Manage → **Deactivate**. They can no longer check in, and
their past records stay for your accounts.

## When someone gets a new phone

Nothing to do — they log in on the new phone with the same mobile number
and PIN. If they forgot the PIN: Staff page → Manage → **Reset PIN**.

## The fingerprint machine

- The machine and the app check each other: a day counts as **Verified**
  when both agree.
- If the dashboard says the machine **"hasn't synced"**, check the
  machine's internet (its Wi-Fi/cable), then its power. Punches made
  while it was offline arrive automatically once it reconnects.
- A new machine appears on the Settings page by itself the first time it
  connects — you just press **Register**.

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "— missing" under Fingerprint | They used the app but not the machine (or machine is offline) | If the machine was working, ask the worker; decide the day on the Attendance page |
| "Check this" on a day | Only one of the two checks happened | Open Attendance, look, decide |
| Worker says app won't let them check in | They are outside the shop area, or location is off on their phone | Ask them to switch on location and stand inside the shop |
| A day is marked Absent but they came | They forgot to punch | Attendance page → that day → decide it as Present |

## Two shops

The switcher in the sidebar changes which shop you are looking at —
Today, Staff, Attendance and Salary all follow it. Each shop has its own
join code and its own fingerprint machine.
