# About the Oriel billing software — a few questions

**Please read first:** nothing here changes anything in Oriel. We are not
installing anything, not editing anything, and not touching your bills.
Everything below is either a question to ask, or something to *look at*
and send us a photo of.

**Why we are asking.** We are building the part of the app where staff
record what they have sold. Oriel already knows every product and every
bill — the only thing it does not know is which staff member made each
sale. If we can read what Oriel already has, then two good things happen:

1. Staff never have to type a product name or price. They point the phone
   at the barcode and the item is recognised on its own.
2. The app can check every sale a staff member claims against the real
   bill, by itself, every night. You would only ever be shown the few
   that do not match, instead of having to check anything yourself.

There are three short parts. **Part 1** is for the Oriel people. **Part 2**
is for you. **Part 3** is a few minutes on the shop computer.

---

## Part 1 — Questions for the Oriel people

You can forward this section to them as it is, or read it out on a call.
Please ask them to answer **by number**, so nothing gets missed.

> **1.** Does Oriel have a way to record *which staff member* made a sale
> — a "salesman", "sales executive" or "staff" box on the bill screen? If
> yes, can we get a report of sales grouped by that person?
>
> **2.** Can Oriel export the full item list — barcode, item name, price,
> and which category or counter it belongs to — as an Excel or CSV file?
>
> **3.** Can Oriel export bills **with the individual items shown on each
> bill**, not just the bill total? We need the barcode, quantity and
> amount for every line of the bill.
>
> **4.** Can Oriel be set to save either of those exports into a folder
> on the computer automatically — say once every evening — without
> somebody having to click it each time?
>
> **5.** Which database does Oriel use to store its data, and where on
> the computer is that data file kept?
>
> **6.** Would you allow a small program of ours to **read** that
> database, without writing anything to it? We want to be sure this does
> not affect your support or warranty in any way. If a password is
> needed, please tell us how to get a read-only one.
>
> **7.** Both our shops run Oriel. Do they share one common database, or
> does each shop keep its own separate one?
>
> **8.** If a bill is cancelled, or a customer returns an item the next
> day, does that show up in the exports — and how would we tell a return
> apart from a normal sale?

**Question 1 is the most important one.** If Oriel can already record who
made the sale, a large part of what we are building may not be needed at
all, and we would rather find that out now.

---

## Part 2 — Two questions only you can answer

**A. When you print barcode stickers, does every single piece get its own
different number, or do all pieces of the same design and size share one
number?**

For example — if you have ten identical blue shirts in size 40, do all
ten stickers read the same, or does each shirt have its own number?

This one matters a lot. If every piece has its own number, the app can
tell on its own when something has been counted twice, with no
guesswork at all.

**B. At the counters, does each staff member handle their own products
only** (one person on sarees, another on kurtas, and so on), **or does
anyone sell anything?**

## Part 3 — Three things to look up on the shop computer

About ten minutes. **Please do not change or delete anything** — we only
need to look.

### 3.1 — Find where Oriel is installed

1. Press the **Windows key** on the keyboard (the one with the four
   squares, near the space bar).
2. Type **Oriel**.
3. Oriel will appear in the list. **Right-click** on it.
4. Choose **Open file location**.
5. A folder window opens. If what you see is an arrow-shaped shortcut
   icon rather than a list of files, right-click it and choose **Open
   file location** once more.
6. *If you can:* click **View** at the top of that window and choose
   **Details**. This adds a column showing what type each file is. If you
   cannot find it, never mind — carry on.
7. **Send us a picture of that window.**

> **How to send a picture:** the easiest way is to simply take a photo of
> the screen with your phone and send it on WhatsApp. That works
> perfectly well. If you would rather do it on the computer, hold
> **Windows key + Shift + S**, drag a box around the window, and it is
> copied ready to paste into an email.

There will be a lot of files with odd names. You do not need to
understand any of them — one of them tells us what we need to know, and
we will pick it out.

### 3.2 — Export the item list

1. Open Oriel as normal.
2. Look for a menu called **Reports**, **Masters**, **Item Master** or
   **Stock** — the wording differs between versions.
3. Find the list of all items, with their barcodes and prices.
4. Look for a button marked **Export**, **Excel** or a small green Excel
   icon, and save the file to the **Desktop**.
5. **Send us that file.**

> If you cannot find it, this is exactly what question 2 in Part 1 is
> for — just ask them and skip this step.

### 3.3 — Export one day's bills

1. In the same **Reports** menu, look for something like **Sales
   Register**, **Bill Register**, **Item-wise Sales** or **Day Book**.
2. Choose **any one ordinary day** of trading — yesterday is fine.
3. **Important:** if it offers a choice between a summary and a detailed
   or item-wise report, choose the **detailed / item-wise** one. We need
   to see the individual items on each bill, not just the totals.
4. Export it to Excel and save it to the **Desktop**.
5. **Send us that file.**

> There is no need to hide or remove anything from these files. They stay
> between us and are only used to work out the format.

- [ ] Your answers to the two questions in Part 2
- [ ] A photo of the Oriel folder (step 3.1)
