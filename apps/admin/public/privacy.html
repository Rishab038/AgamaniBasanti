Privacy Policy — Agamani Staff

Last updated 28 July 2026

Agamani Staff is a private app for people employed by Agamani Basanti Fashions Pvt. Ltd. (Krishnanagar and Kanchrapara, West Bengal). It records attendance and, for staff working the billing counter, the shop's credit book. It is not a consumer app and has no public sign-up: an account is created only by the shop owner, or by a worker whose request the owner approves.

What the app collects, and why

InformationWhyKept for
Your name and mobile number Your login, and to identify you on the attendance and salary records While you are employed, and afterwards as an employment record
Your location, at the moment you punch To confirm you are at the shop. The app does not track you at any other time and does not run in the background. With the punch record
A photo of you at check-in Only when the location check is unclear, so the owner can confirm you were at the shop. Optional — you can check in without it. Deleted automatically after 2 days
Times of your check-in, lunch and check-out Attendance and salary As an employment record
Your fingerprint enrolment number To match the shop's fingerprint machine to your record. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint itself — that stays on the machine. While you are employed
Salary, advances and payslips To pay you, and so you can see your own payslip As an accounting record
A device identifier So one account is used from one phone, which prevents someone punching in on your behalf Until the owner resets it
A notification token To send you shift reminders and answers to advance requests Until you sign out or reinstall

Customer information (billing counter staff only)

Staff the owner has authorised for the billing counter also record, on the shop's behalf, the details of customers buying on credit: name, phone number, bill and amount owed, photographs of the bill, and any proof of payment. This is the shop's own business record, held for as long as the debt and the accounts require. Customers with questions about their entry should contact the shop directly.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own attendance, payslips and advances.
  • The shop owner can see all of it — that is the purpose of the system.
  • Other staff cannot see your records. This is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to any third party for their own purposes.

Where it is stored

On Supabase (Postgres and file storage, Mumbai region, India) and delivered over Cloudflare. Notifications are sent through Expo and, on Android, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process the data only to run the service. Everything travels over an encrypted connection.

Your consent

Before your first check-in the app shows you, in plain language, what it collects and why, and records that you have seen it. This is required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Your rights

You can ask the shop owner to show you what is held about you, correct anything wrong, or delete your account. Attendance and salary records that the shop must keep for accounting or statutory reasons (PF, ESI, professional tax) may be retained even after your account is removed.

Children

The app is for employed adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes are told to staff directly.
